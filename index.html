AERODYNAMICS, REDEFINED

Replacing the previous three-radiator concept with a central radiator layout creates space on the sides for active aerodynamic elements.

01 // REAR AERO

SWAN-NECK ACTIVE WING

The top-mounted swan-neck pylons minimize turbulence under the wing surface where up to 70% of downforce is generated. Features DRS (Drag Reduction System).

POWERTRAIN MASTERY

FLAT-SIX

A compact high-performance naturally aspirated flat-six architecture engineered for extreme response, uncompromising power delivery and motorsport reliability up to 9,000 RPM.

01

INDIVIDUAL THROTTLE BODIES

Six separate throttle butterflies provide telepathic throttle response and optimized cylinder charge filling.

02

RIGID VALVE DRIVE

No hydraulic lash adjusters. Rocker arms operate directly on the valves with shims, tested up to 9,000 RPM continuous track load.

03

DRY SUMP WITH 7 SUCTION STAGES

Reliable oil supply even under sustained lateral acceleration exceeding 1.6 G. Oil centrifugally defoamed in a separate tank.

04

TITANIUM CONNECTING RODS

Forged lightweight titanium rods reduce oscillating mass, enabling explosive rev increases and race-grade durability.
